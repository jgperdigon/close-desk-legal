Privacy Policy

Close Desk · Bay Area Trainers And Advisors LLC · Effective November 7, 2026

Who we are

Close Desk is a private business application operated by Bay Area Trainers And Advisors LLC ("we", "us"). It is used by our own staff to run our business. This policy explains what information Close Desk handles and how.

Information we handle

  • Account information for staff who sign in (name, email, sign-in credentials managed by our authentication provider).
  • Customer and contact information our business works with: names, phone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, organizations, appointment details, services and prices.
  • Communications: call records, call recordings and transcripts, and text messages exchanged with our business phone numbers.
  • Connected accounts: when we connect Google Calendar or QuickBooks Online, the access tokens those services issue, and the calendar events and accounting records (customers, products and services, invoices) needed for the features we use.

How we use it

Only to operate our business: answering and following up on calls and messages, booking and managing appointments, keeping calendars up to date, and creating invoices in our QuickBooks Online company when a staff member chooses to send one. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

QuickBooks Online

When QuickBooks Online is connected, Close Desk reads the company's product/service and customer lists so staff can link them, and creates customers and invoices in that company when a staff member taps "Send to QuickBooks". QuickBooks access tokens are stored encrypted on our servers, are never sent to the mobile app, and are used only for these actions. Disconnecting QuickBooks in the app revokes access at Intuit and deletes the stored tokens. Invoices already created remain in QuickBooks.

Service providers

We rely on service providers who process information on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication and server functions), Twilio (phone calls and text messages), OpenAI (call transcription and summaries), Google (Calendar and address lookup), Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and Expo (app delivery and notifications). Each receives only what its function requires.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, access is limited to our business's signed-in staff, and third-party tokens are encrypted at rest. We keep information as long as needed to run our business and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, then delete it.

Your choices
