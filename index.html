Close Desk

Operated by Bay Area Trainers And Advisors LLC

Close Desk is a private business app for managing calls, customers, follow-ups and bookings. It is used by Bay Area Trainers And Advisors LLC and is not offered to the public.

When connected, Close Desk can send invoices for booked appointments to the business's own QuickBooks Online company. Connecting and disconnecting QuickBooks is done from Settings → Integrations in the app.
